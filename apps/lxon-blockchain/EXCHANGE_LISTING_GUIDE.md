## Exchange-Specific Applications

### Binance Application
**Requirements**:
- Project completion and mainnet launch
- Smart contract audit by reputable firm
- Daily trading volume > $1M (tiered)
- Community engagement (Twitter, Telegram, Discord)
- Professional team with doxxed members
- Legal compliance in target jurisdictions

**Application Process**:
1. Submit through Binance Listing Application Form
2. Provide all required documentation
3. Wait for review (4-8 weeks)
4. Respond to additional information requests
5. Due diligence and compliance review
6. Final approval and listing

**Listing Fee**: ~$50,000 - $100,000
**Timeline**: 8-12 weeks from application to listing

### Coinbase Application
**Requirements**:
- US regulatory compliance (SEC, FinCEN)
- Established legal entity in US jurisdiction
- Comprehensive KYC/AML program
- Professional audit from top-tier firm
- Strong community and use case
- Established team with track record

**Application Process**:
1. Submit through Coinbase Asset Application
2. Extensive due diligence process
3. Legal and compliance review
4. Technical security assessment
5. Business model evaluation
6. Final approval (very selective)

**Listing Fee**: ~$100,000 - $500,000
**Timeline**: 12-24 weeks (highly selective)

### Kraken Application
**Requirements**:
- Strong technical foundation
- Professional audit
- Legal compliance
- Community support
- Unique value proposition

**Application Process**:
1. Submit through Kraken Listing Request
2. Technical and security review
3. Legal compliance assessment
4. Business evaluation
5. Community due diligence
6. Final approval

**Listing Fee**: ~$25,000 - $75,000
**Timeline**: 6-10 weeks

### DEX Listing (Uniswap)
**Requirements**:
- ERC-20 compatible or bridged token
- Liquidity pool creation
- No listing fee
- Immediate listing possible

**Application Process**:
1. Create liquidity pool on Uniswap
2. Add initial liquidity (min $50k recommended)
3. List on token discovery platforms
4. Community awareness campaign
5. Ongoing liquidity management

**Listing Fee**: $0 (gas fees only)
**Timeline**: Immediate
